# Show monster skills and dungeon info beside the game
Labels: priority: medium, enhancement

**Why:** checking a monster's skills or a dungeon's mechanics means leaving the game for fan sites.

**Scope:**
- Companion tab, read-only: identifies the monster or dungeon on screen (OCR) and shows its skills or
  floor mechanics from fan data.
- Suggests profile rules from skill keywords (e.g. "voids damage absorb").
- Out: writing those rules into profiles (issue 4 does editing).

**Done when:** pressing-and-holding a team member's portrait shows that monster's skills in the tab
within a few seconds.
